Services

Our Legal Services

  • Administration of Deceased Estates

    We assist families in navigating the legal process of winding up a loved one’s estate. Our team ensures all estate documents are correctly completed, assets are identified, debts are settled, and distributions are carried out in accordance with the law. We provide compassionate, structured support during a difficult time, ensuring compliance with the Master of the High Court.

  • Civil Litigation

    We represent individuals and businesses in civil disputes, providing strategic litigation solutions that protect your rights and interests. Whether defending a claim or initiating legal action, we focus on achieving favourable outcomes through negotiation, mediation, or court processes.

  • Wills & Trusts

    We provide professional drafting of Wills and establishment of Trusts to ensure your assets are protected and distributed according to your wishes. Our guidance helps you structure your estate efficiently, avoiding future disputes and ensuring long-term financial security for your family.

  • Criminal Law

    We defend clients facing criminal charges, ensuring that their constitutional rights are protected throughout the legal process. From bail applications and police station consultations to trial representation, we handle all criminal matters with skill and urgency.

  • Labour Law

    We assist both employers and employees with CCMA matters, unfair dismissals, labour disputes, disciplinary hearings, workplace policies, and employment contracts. Our aim is to resolve labour issues swiftly and fairly.

  • RAF Claims

    We help victims of road accidents pursue compensation through the Road Accident Fund. From medical assessment coordination to claim lodgment and litigation, we ensure every claim is handled with attention to detail to maximise compensation.

  • Evictions

    Our firm assists landlords and property owners with lawful and efficient eviction processes. We follow the provisions of the PIE Act to ensure evictions are handled fairly, legally, and without unnecessary delays.

  • Divorce

    We provide compassionate but firm guidance through divorce proceedings, whether contested or uncontested. Our goal is to protect your rights, secure fair settlements, and ensure the best outcome for all parties involved.

Fees

Transparent Fee Structure

Our fees for estate administration are charged in line with the tariff prescribed by the Master’s Office. The fee structure is transparent, and clients are informed of costs upfront based on the value and complexity of the estate.

Fees are based on the nature of the dispute , required court appearances, and preparation time. Fixed-fee consultations and hourly billing options are available depending on case complexity.

Will drafting is offered at a fixed fee, while Trust setup costs depend on the structure and administrative requirements. Affordable packages are available for long-term estate planning.

Criminal law fees vary depending on the stage of the matter bail applications, consultations, and trial work each carry different costs. Clients receive a full breakdown before engagement.

Labour law services may be charged per consultation, per hearing, or on a fixed-fee basis for specific matters such as drafting disciplinary charges or representation at the CCMA.

RAF claims are handled on a contingency fee basis—meaning no payment is required unless compensation is successfully recovered, within the limits of the Contingency Fees Act.

Eviction fees depend on whether the matter is uncontested or contested. A fixed-fee option is available for straightforward eviction applications.

Uncontested divorces are offered at a fixed fee, while contested matters are billed based on time, preparation, and court appearances. A cost estimate is provided at the start of each case.
